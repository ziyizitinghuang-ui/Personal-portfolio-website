Selected Project

Soft Noise — Interactive Journaling Website

Soft Noise is a website project centered around mood, journaling, and emotional expression. It was designed as a gentle space where users can choose a mood, write down their thoughts, and look back on past entries.

In this project, I worked on:

  • planning the mood-based user flow
  • designing the visual style and page layout
  • building parts of the website with HTML, CSS, and basic JavaScript
  • thinking about how interaction and design can make the experience feel softer and more comfortable
